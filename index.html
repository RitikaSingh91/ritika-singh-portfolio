01 — About

A little about me

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student, and honestly, I fell into programming almost by accident — I liked the idea of building things that actually work, and computer science felt like the subject where "figuring it out" is basically the whole job. That's what kept me here.

These days a lot of my time goes into Data Structures & Algorithms — not because it's glamorous, but because it's taught me to think before I type. Alongside that, I'm learning to build full applications with the MERN stack, which means going from "I understand this concept" to "I shipped something that works end to end."

I care about building real things over collecting certificates, so most of my learning shows up as projects rather than just notes. Long term, I want to work as a software developer on products that solve problems people actually have — and I try to stay honest about what I know versus what I'm still learning, instead of pretending otherwise.

Outside of code, I'm usually creating something — a reel, a recipe, a bit of DIY, or documenting whatever I'm learning that week. It keeps me balanced, and it's taught me a lot about communication, which turns out to matter just as much as code.
